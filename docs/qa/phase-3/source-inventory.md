## Setup process source

Approved design PDF, page 10: understand the business → demo/quote → configuration → installation/training → launch support → ongoing support. The customer-facing step text is:
- **Understand your business:** Walk through your current checkout, menu or inventory and the way your team works.
- **Demo and quote:** See the proposed system and talk through the costs.
- **Configuration:** Plan the menu, inventory and payment settings your business needs.
- **Installation and training:** Get the hardware set up and walk your team through using it.
- **Launch support:** Talk through the move to your new system and the help you need on launch day.
- **Ongoing support:** Keep the support contact details handy for questions after launch.

- RCKT mobile POS: name retained per the explicit migration requirement; no reader, phone, network or receipt specification is inferred on the mobile page.
- Clover Mini and Station Duo: names retained at their mapped terminal destination with a link to the POS comparison. Their features are not assumed to be those of a terminal.
- Gateways: the shared reporting/QuickBooks bullet list is not assigned independently to each named gateway.
- Biller Genie/MantaPay: no copied vendor statistics, preferred-partner claim, setup-time promise or fee-elimination language. QuickBooks editions, ACH and scheduled billing remain questions for the actual plan.

### / — rate-lock

A markup that stays put We lock our rate. TruePay won't raise its markup for as long as you process with us. What this covers Covers TruePay's own markup and monthly fees. Card-network interchange and assessments are set by Visa, Mastercard, Discover and Amex and can change for every processor; we pass those through at cost and show them on your statement. What it doesn’t The rate lock does not prevent card networks from changing interchange and assessments. Ask for the written terms before choosing. Read the commitment terms
